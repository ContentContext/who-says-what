Youth issues: Who says what?

“Youth Issues: Who says what?” is a simple, non-partisan tool that compares the positions and views on relevant topics to youth of political parties running for the European Elections 2014.

The tool encompasses European as well as national political parties and gives you the opportunity to compare issues between parties and countries on themes like education, employment, digital rights, the Future of Europe, political participation, the Environment and Investment in Youth.

Once you know what they promise, it’s time to make your choice!

Click to expand and make a selection.
