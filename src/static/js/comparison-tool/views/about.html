“Youth Issues: Who says what?” is a simple tool that compares the positions and views of political parties running for the European Elections 2014.

The tool has been created by the League of Young Voters in Europe in cooperation with Kieskompas.

Disclaimer and Q&A

The League of Young Voters in Europe is a non-partisan initiative and is in no way affiliated to political parties or the European institutions. The content of this tool has been extracted from official manifestos for the 2014 European Parliament elections.

Why Youth Issues?

A study by the League of Young Voters found that during the 2009 European Elections, young people felt that they did not have sufficient information about the elections and its content. In 2009, 71% of the eligible youth (18-24) abstained from the elections, resulting in the underrepresentation of young people in the European Parliament.

This tool aims to present all the statements made by political parties on issues of relevance to youth so that young people can have a simple overview on issues that concern them.

What topics did you look at and how were they selected?

The topics included in this tool are: Education, Employment, Digital Revolution, Political Participation, the Future of Europe, Investment in Youth and the Environment. The topics were selected from young people's input on the League of Young Voters website in addition to the long standing expertise of the European Youth Forum.

Why are some political parties left blank?

For this tool, only official manifestos for the 2014 European Elections were considered. If a particular party is not portrayed in this tool, it is because they did not issue a manifesto for these elections.

The research for this tool was done at the end of March/early April, therefore any manifesto published after this date will not have been considered. Similarly, if a party has not elaborated a statement or position on a topic selected by the League, the space is left blank.

Which countries are included?

All EU Member States except for Malta. The tool comprises national political parties and European political parties.

Where does the content come from?

All text excerpts are the original text from the political parties’ manifestos. We therefore do not take responsibility for the content.

I noticed mistakes for my country

The collection of content for this tool constituted a major effort and the tool comprises a lot of content. We verified everything the best we could, but if you have noticed a mistake for the content of your country, please write us at hello@youngvoters.eu and we will amend any errors.
